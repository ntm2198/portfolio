Reading Reflection (taken from slack)

With a class coming in from so many different disciplines, some technical, some educational, and some on the UX/Design side of things, I enjoyed that the 3 readings for this week laid a framework to connect these disciplines, undertanding pedagogical theory (Behaviorism, Cognitivism, Constructivism) as the framework to which we can apply design thinking (What is design knowledge and how do we teach it?) to tackle the "wicked problem" of education through the medium of tangible tools (Tangibles and Tangible Learning). Its a tall task to be interdisiplinary in a way that pushes us out of our comfort zone, especially when many of our careers are focused on the theorical "behind the scenes" aspects of education, tech, edTech, design, etc. I've recently been grappling with the disconnect between the work done on edTech platforms by designers, researchers, etc and seeing on the ground teachers really struggle to adopt or effectively use these tools while under pressure to stay trained on effective classroom management, lesson planning, working with school administrations, etc. (this is at a primary school level) Its a tension I hope we can dive in to as the class goes on! Nameley, how do we create tangibles that are easy for teachers to teach with and learners to learn from?
